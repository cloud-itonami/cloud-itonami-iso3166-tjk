# Operator Guide

## First Deployment

1. Confirm the client's incorporation/legal-entity status is complete
   (route to `cloud-itonami-M6910` or local counsel first if not).
2. Register the client's intake: business type, target public function,
   prior filing history in Tajikistan if any.
3. Run the advisor in read-only mode against the Agency on Public
   Procurement of Goods, Works and Services' electronic procurement
   system.
4. Compare the checklist against the client's current documentation
   (Tax Committee Unified state register status, Law "On Public
   Procurement of Goods, Works and Services" (2006) compliance record,
   Tax Code (2012) tax-registration record).
5. If the engagement involves government interests (e.g. a Free
   Economic Zone), route to the State Committee on Investments and
   State Property Management for foreign-investment-screening clearance
   under Law No. 2173 "On Capital and Promotion of Investment Activity"
   (14 May 2025) and Government Decree No. 590 (28 December 2006)
   BEFORE any filing submission -- this is a HARD governor gate, not
   optional.
6. **Never cite the Agency for State Financial Control and Combating
   Corruption as the procurement regulator.** It is a wholly separate
   anti-corruption oversight body. Any assessment that conflates it
   with the Agency on Public Procurement is rejected unconditionally by
   the governor's flagship check.
7. Enable gated filing-draft assistance once the Market-Entry Compliance
   Governor contract is trusted; actual submission always requires human
   sign-off.

## Minimum Production Controls

- client-owned data store for business/tax registration documents
- clear provenance (official portal/regulation citation) for every
  requirement surfaced
- approval workflow for any portal registration or filing submission
- named referral relationship with Tajikistan-licensed counsel or a
  registered agent for anything beyond checklist/draft assistance
- monthly audit export

## Certification

Certified operators must prove data provenance, audit traceability, that
automated actions cannot bypass the Market-Entry Compliance Governor,
that the procurement regulator is never confused with the anti-
corruption agency, and a working referral relationship with Tajikistan-
licensed counsel or a registered agent for whatever licensed
representation the law of Tajikistan requires for actual
public-procurement filings.
